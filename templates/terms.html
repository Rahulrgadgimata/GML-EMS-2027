Terms & Conditions

By registering as a player or a team owner you agree to the following.

  1. Accurate information. Every detail you submit — name, USN, email, contact and achievements — must be true. False entries are removed from the auction pool without notice.
  2. One entry per USN, per sport. You may register for more than one sport, but not twice for the same one.
  3. Eligibility. Players must be currently enrolled students of the participating colleges and must carry a valid college ID on match day.
  4. Auction purse. Team owners bid against a fixed budget. A bid above the remaining purse is rejected by the system, and all sales are final unless reset by the League Admin.
  5. Admin authority. The League Admin may reset a bid, correct player details or reallocate a player where an error or dispute is proven.
  6. Conduct. Misuse of the platform, collusion during the auction, abusive behaviour or any breach of the rules leads to disqualification of the player or the franchise.
  7. Photos. Uploaded images must be of the registered player or team and appropriate for public display. Anything else is deleted.
  8. Communication. Registration confirmations and auction results are emailed to the address you provide. Keeping it correct and monitored is your responsibility.
  9. Data use. Your details are used solely to run {{ brand.name }} — team allocation, scheduling and league communication — and are not shared outside the organising committee.
